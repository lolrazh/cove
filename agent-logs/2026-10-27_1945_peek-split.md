## User Intention
Section 5f of `ARC-PARITY.md`. Little Arc: a setting to open links from
other apps in a small window, with Open in Cove moving the live tab into
the last active window, and ⌥⌘N for an empty one; `ExternalURLRouter`
stays the one path, with a clean decision point for Air Traffic Control.
Peek: links from Favorites and Pinned tabs to another site open in a card
over the page, with expand and close, Escape closing; a modifier for any
link. Split View: up to four tabs, resizable dividers, focus follows
clicks with a subtle highlight, ⌃⇧=, drag to the page edge, Open in Split
View, one grouped entry in the sidebar and strip, closing a pane unsplits,
saved with the session, and page tools, ⌘L, navigation and site controls
acting on the focused pane. Design the active-tab model explicitly and
keep TabManager's diff focused.

## What We Accomplished

### Shared groundwork
- ✅ **`WebViewRepresentable` holds the web view in a container.** SwiftUI
  only adds and removes containers, so a web view can move between places
  (Peek → page, pane → pane, Little Cove → browser window) without a
  reload. A container takes its web view once, when made or handed a
  different one: a Peek card still on screen in its closing transition
  otherwise took the page back, leaving the expanded tab blank.
- ✅ **`TabSession.Host`** replaces the fixed `onSelect`/`onOpenInNewTab`
  callbacks: `select`, `openInNewTab` and an optional `peek`. A tab that
  moves gets a new host (`TabManager.adoptTab`) and keeps its page.
- ✅ **`PageView`**: one tab's page with its error page, loading line, link
  preview and find bar, moved out of `BrowserShellView` so the window,
  each split pane, the Peek and Little Cove all use it. **`chromeCard()`**
  is the card's clip, hit area and shadow, shared the same way.

### Little Cove (Arc's Little Arc)
- ✅ Setting `browser.opensLinksInLittleCove`, Settings › General › Links.
- ✅ `LittleCove` (owned by `AppServices`) opens an AppKit window per page:
  760×600, cascaded, traffic lights, a bar with favicon and domain ("Little
  Cove" on the start page) and **Open in Cove**, over the page in a card.
  Open in Cove closes the window and hands the tab to
  `ExternalURLRouter.moveToBrowserWindow`, which adopts it into the last
  active window's Today (replacing an untouched start page). Disabled on
  the start page. Pages asking for a new window go to Cove.
- ✅ ⌥⌘N (File › New Little Cove Window) and the Command Bar's New Little
  Cove Window, from anywhere in the app.
- ✅ **Router**: `open(_:from: .otherApp | .cove)` is the decision point:
  a link from another app goes to Little Cove if the setting is on, else a
  tab. History passes `.cove`. Air Traffic Control slots in at the same
  place. Pending work became closures, so a tab moved with every window
  closed waits too; the router keeps a browser window's `openWindow` to
  open one in that case.
- ✅ ⌘W closes a window that isn't a browser window (Little Cove, History),
  instead of being disabled there.

### Peek
- ✅ `TabSession.opensInPeek`: a link activation (or a new-window link) that
  was ⇧-clicked, or comes from a pinned tab or favorite and leads off its
  site (`Site.contains`), goes to `host.peek` and the navigation is
  cancelled.
- ✅ `TabManager+Peek`: `peek` holds a tab in no list, built with a host
  that opens new-tab links beside the opener; `closePeek`, `expandPeek`
  (`adoptTab` beside the opener). Selecting another tab closes the Peek.
- ✅ `PeekOverlay`: the page dimmed, a card inset 48/28pt with the panel
  shadow; site, Open as Tab, Close (`.cancelAction`, so Escape works with
  the peeked page focused). Spring scale-in, a fade under Reduce Motion.

### Split View
- ✅ **Model (`TabSplit<Pane>`, pure, tested):** two to four panes along a
  `SplitAxis`, each with a share. `insert` evens the shares, `removing`
  and `keeping` grow the rest in proportion and return nil below two
  panes, `neighbor(of:)`, `dividers`, `moveDivider` (neither pane below
  15%), and `map` for saving by Today index. `TabEntry` is a tab or a
  split, and `tabs.entries(splits:)` shows a split once, where its first
  tab is, in pane order.
- ✅ **The active-tab model:** `activeTabID` is the focused pane; the
  selected entry is derived (`activeSplit`, the split holding it). So
  everything that acts on the active tab (page tools, ⌘L, navigation, the
  URL pill, site controls, Boosts) acts on the focused pane with no
  change. `shownTabs` are all woken in `activeTabID`'s `didSet`;
  `isShown` keeps every visible pane out of the sleep and archive sweeps.
- ✅ **TabManager** (main file: storage and invariants only):
  `@Published splits`, pruned in `tabs`' `didSet`, so a tab that is
  closed, archived or pinned leaves its split and a split of one
  dissolves. Closing the focused pane focuses its neighbor. Dragging a
  split member moves the whole group (`Array.move(_:onto:)` for groups),
  new tabs open past the opener's split, and `selectTab` ignores the tab
  already selected. Everything else is in `TabManager+SplitView`:
  `openInSplitView(_:at:)` (after the focused pane, or leading/trailing),
  `addSplitView` (⌃⇧=), `addTabInSplitView(url:)`, `separateSplit`,
  `moveSplitDivider`, `rotateSplit`. Splits are Today-only.
- ✅ **Session and Spaces:** splits belong to a space's Today: a window's
  parked spaces keep theirs (`ParkedSpace.splits`), switching spaces swaps
  them with the tabs, and `SavedSpace.splits: [TabSplit<Int>]?` saves them
  by Today index (absent in older files). Split changes trigger a save.
- ✅ **`SplitPageView`:** panes side by side or stacked, each its own card
  with an 8pt shell-colored gap that is also the divider (column/row
  resize pointer). Focus: `BrowserWebView.onMouseDown` calls the tab's
  `host.select()` (SwiftUI gestures never see clicks in a web view), plus a
  simultaneous tap for start pages. The focused pane gets a 2pt tint
  outline at 60%.
- ✅ **Drag to split:** the dragged tab moved from the sidebar's and
  strip's `@State` into `TabManager.draggedTabID`, so the page can see it.
  While a Today tab that can join is dragged, the page's leading and
  trailing quarters accept it, previewing the pane in the tint.
- ✅ **Sidebar and strip:** Today renders `todayEntries`; a split is one row
  of `ChromeTabItem` segments on a resting fill, the focused one raised.
  In the strip the segments keep their usual widths, grouped the same way,
  so the width math is unchanged.
- ✅ **Menus and Command Bar:** a tab's menu offers Open in Split View or
  Separate Split View; the Tab menu has Add Split View ⌃⇧=, Stack / Side by
  Side, Separate. The Command Bar has a Split View mode (its glyph and
  "Open in Split View" placeholder; Return opens a page in a new pane or
  brings an open tab in) and commands Add, Rotate, Separate Split View.
- ✅ **Boosts (rebased on #14):** the editor and Zap already act on
  `activePage`, the focused pane. The panel now shows over the pane it
  edits (`BoostEditorOverlay`, on the single page and on each pane) rather
  than over the whole page area, and sits beneath the Peek's dim.

## Key Learnings
- **A view in a removal transition is still live.** Its `updateNSView`
  runs, so a representable that re-claims a shared `NSView` on update
  steals it back from the view replacing it. Claim once per instance.
- **SwiftUI's `.saveItem` placement doesn't exist on macOS 27's File
  menu**; anything placed relative to it silently vanishes. List the menu
  with System Events (`AXMenuItemCmdChar`) to see what's really there.
- **`postToPid` keys go to the app, not necessarily the window you think**;
  for a second window, a System Events keystroke after a frontmost check
  was reliable.
- **Logging from the sandboxed app**: `open --stdout` captured nothing and
  `log show` showed nothing here; a temporary file in the container
  (`~/Library/Containers/com.cove.browser/Data/`) worked.

## Decisions for the coordinator
- **Splits hold Today tabs only.** A pinned tab or favorite can't join
  (the menu items are hidden or disabled), which keeps the grouped entry in
  one section and the session format simple. Arc allows pinned splits.
- **⇧-click is Peek's modifier** for any link (Safari uses it for Reading
  List, which Cove doesn't have).
- **Little Cove is a normal-level window**, not floating above other apps.
  A cold launch from a link with the setting on still opens the browser
  window SwiftUI makes at launch, plus Little Cove.
- **The focused pane's outline uses the tint**, the one outline in the
  chrome, as a focus indicator.
- ⚠️ **Stray text in the user's history.** Near the end, the user typed
  while this build was frontmost; their text landed in the Command Bar
  (open in Split View mode for a test) and ran as a Google search in a new
  pane. It's in the shared `history.db` and the session. I quit the
  instance, deleted those two screenshots, and left the history alone for
  the user or coordinator to clear.
